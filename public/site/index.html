Good to know

Who can stay at Ryuu?

Ryuu is a men's PG hostel for men aged 18 and above, both students and working professionals.

What rooms are available?

There are three room types: three sharing (200 sq ft), four sharing (300 sq ft) and five sharing (440 sq ft), all air-conditioned. Every room has a balcony and an attached bathroom with hot water, and every resident gets a steel cot with a six-inch foam mattress and a steel cupboard with a safety locker. You can stay daily, weekly or monthly.

What food is served?

Homemade food every day: veg and non-veg options every day, and eggs available every morning. Residents choose the next day's breakfast, lunch and dinner in their account between 6 PM and 11 PM.

Is it safe?

Yes. There is 24/7 security staff and CCTV surveillance in the common areas, and the portal records attendance and outpasses and keeps guardians informed.

How does admission work?

Create an account, verify your contacts and select your preferred bed. An admin reviews your application. Once approved, submit your first month's rent and refundable deposit details within 48 hours. Your booking is confirmed after payment verification. For bulk or individual bookings, you can also call our admissions team.

Where is it, and when does it open?

No. 00, 80 Feet Road, 5th Block, Koramangala, Bengaluru, close to Hosur Road, Sarjapur Road, HSR Layout and Indiranagar. Ryuu is opening shortly.
